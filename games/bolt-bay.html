About the game

Tap a screw to unscrew it and watch it fly into the toolbox of its color. Fill a toolbox with three matching screws and it ships out.

  • 10,000 levels, from relaxing warm-ups to extreme brain teasers, each checked by a solver
  • Locked toolboxes, frozen screws, glass panes and chained plates
  • A color-blind mode that adds a shape to every screw head
  • Plays offline, with no account needed

Free to play, with ads and optional purchases, including a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Every level can be finished without paying.
